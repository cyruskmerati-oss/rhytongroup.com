What CliniCall does

Built for the front office of a busy practice, where every call needs to be written up accurately.

Voicemail transcription

Messages left for the practice are transcribed automatically, so staff can read a voicemail instead of replaying it.

Draft notes for review

A draft chief complaint and history is prepared from what the patient said, ready for staff to check and edit.

A person reviews every note

Nothing is passed along automatically. Every draft is read and approved by a member of staff first.

English and Farsi

Designed for bilingual practices, including English translation of messages left in Farsi.
